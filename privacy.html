Privacy Policy

Effective date: [Effective date: ____]

Welcome Home (listed in app stores as "MyWelcomeHome") is a free app that helps people returning to the community in Fairfield County, Connecticut find local resources, work through reentry checklists, and keep their own notes and reminders. Yale University researchers developed the app and a research study that uses the app to examine [Study goals]. The app is provided by [Organization legal name] ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles, how it is used for the study, and the choices you have.

The app and study are intended for people in the United States who are over 18. Your participation in the study and your use of the app are entirely voluntary.

Research Study and Consent

If you choose to participate in the study, the app will show you an informed consent document that explains the study and how your information will be collected, used, and processed. Please read it carefully. You will be asked to agree in the app before participating.

You do not have to participate in the study to use the app. If you participate, your study data is tied to your account; it is not anonymous. Researchers will use it only for the study. They will combine it with data from other participants for analysis and transfer the combined study data to the Principal Investigator. Study findings may be reported in scientific publications, but only as combined results that do not identify you.

The Principal Investigator is [Principal Investigator name and contact].

Your Account and Sign-In

Welcome Home is invitation-only: you set up your account from a link in an invitation email. Your account needs your email address and a password. You can change your password at any time from the Profile page, and reset a forgotten password from a link we email you.

You can turn on two-step sign-in for extra security. With it on, after you enter your password we email you a single-use code that you must also enter. We offer this when you set up your account, and you can turn it on or off at any time from the Profile page. Turning it on signs out your other devices. Codes expire after ten minutes, and after several wrong codes or passwords in a row, sign-in is paused for a while to protect your account.

Information We Collect

Only what the app needs, and tied to your account:

  • Account information: your email address and password, and the optional first name and profile photo you choose for your Profile page.
  • Your set-up answers: whether you tell us you are new to the app, and the topics you choose so we can show relevant checklists. You can change these from your Profile.
  • Checklist progress: the checklist items you complete and any items you add yourself.
  • Your Files: the photos, voice recordings, and notes you save.
  • Your choices: a record of which version of this policy and the terms you accepted.

We never ask for your legal name, phone number, or location. We do not use analytics or advertising tools, and this website does not use cookies or trackers.

Camera, Microphone, and Photos Permissions

The app asks for these permissions only when you use a related feature, and you can refuse or change them at any time in your device settings. The rest of the app continues to work without them.

  • Camera: to take a photo to attach to a checklist item.
  • Photo library: to choose an existing photo to attach to a checklist item.
  • Microphone: to record a voice note on a checklist item.

Photos and recordings you attach are saved to your account so you can get to them again. The app does not access your camera, photos, or microphone in the background.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to run the app for you: to sign you in, to save and show your checklists, Files, photos, and recordings, and to keep your account secure. If you ask, the app can read the text in a photo you saved, so you can keep or copy it as words. If you consent to the study, the study team also uses your study data as described above. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Researchers will not access or use photographs you save in the app. We will view or access a photograph only if you, as its owner, explicitly ask us to do so to provide technical assistance.

Data Storage and Security

Your account, checklists, and Files are stored on our server, separately for each account: only you can see them in the app. Information travels between the app and our server over encrypted connections. On the server, your information can be reached only by the small, named team that operates the app, and only where needed to run it, support you, and conduct the study if you consent. We use physical, administrative, and technical safeguards designed to protect your information. However, sending information over the internet is never completely secure, and we cannot guarantee that unauthorized access or circumvention of security measures will never occur.

Our server keeps minimal technical logs, such as the time of a request, only to operate and secure the app. These logs do not contain request or response contents or email addresses.

Reminders you set are scheduled locally on your device. They are not sent to or stored on our server.

The app can be used offline in read-only mode, showing information previously loaded on your device. Changes require an internet connection.

Third Parties

Your information is handled only by us and, as service providers, the company that hosts our server and the service that delivers our emails (invitations, password resets, and sign-in codes). They process information only to provide those services. We do not share your information with anyone else except as described in this policy.

Where permitted by law, we may disclose information to comply with state or federal law, a court order, subpoena, or warrant, or to cooperate with law enforcement or another government agency. We may also disclose information when we believe in good faith that it is appropriate or necessary to protect against liability; prevent fraudulent, abusive, or unlawful activity; investigate or defend against claims; protect the security or integrity of the app, the study, or the systems used to provide them; enforce our agreements or protect other legal rights; or protect the rights, property, or safety of any person. When the law permits, we will notify you of such a disclosure.

Do Not Track

We do not track you over time or across third-party websites or online services for targeted advertising. Because we do not perform that kind of tracking, the app and this website do not specifically respond to browser Do Not Track signals.

Children's Privacy

Welcome Home and the study are intended for people over 18. They are not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Leaving the Study

You may withdraw from the study at any time by contacting the App project manager at [App project manager email]. You may keep using the app for your own purposes. After you withdraw, new information you enter in the app will not be used for the study. Information submitted for the study before you withdrew will be retained and may continue to be used for the study as described in this policy.

Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. You may ask us to delete your account and everything tied to it — your profile, checklists, Files, photos, and recordings — by contacting us at the address below. You may also ask us to delete an individual photograph by contacting the App project manager. We will complete a deletion request within a reasonable time, except where we must keep information to meet a legal obligation or where study information submitted before withdrawal is retained as described above. Deletion is final and cannot be undone. Removing the app from your device also removes the reminders and other data stored on the device.

Your Rights

You may ask to see the information we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable law. To make a request, contact us below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the new version on this page and change the effective date above. If a change materially affects your rights or obligations, we will make reasonable efforts to notify you and show a pop-up or similar notice in the app the first time you use it after the change. Please review the updated policy carefully.
