Privacy Policy

Welcome Home (listed in app stores as "MyWelcomeHome") is a free app that helps people returning to the community in Fairfield County, Connecticut find local resources, work through reentry checklists, and keep their own notes and reminders. The app is provided by [Organization legal name] ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles and the choices you have.

Your Account and Sign-In

Welcome Home is invitation-only: you set up your account from a link in an invitation email. Your account needs your email address and a password. You can change your password at any time from the Profile page, and reset a forgotten password from a link we email you.

You can turn on two-step sign-in for extra security. With it on, after you enter your password we email you a single-use code that you must also enter. We offer this when you set up your account, and you can turn it on or off at any time from the Profile page. Turning it on signs out your other devices. Codes expire after ten minutes, and after several wrong codes or passwords in a row, sign-in is paused for a while to protect your account.

Information We Collect

Only what the app needs, and tied to your account:

  • Account information: your email address and password, and the optional first name and profile photo you choose for your Profile page.
  • Your set-up answers: whether you tell us you are new to the app, and the topics you choose so we can show relevant checklists. You can change these from your Profile.
  • Checklist progress: the checklist items you complete and any items you add yourself.
  • Your Files: the photos, voice recordings, and notes you save.
  • Your choices: a record of which version of this policy and the terms you accepted.

We never ask for your legal name, phone number, or location. We do not use analytics or advertising tools, and this website does not use cookies or trackers.

Camera, Microphone, and Photos Permissions

The app asks for these permissions only when you use a related feature, and you can refuse or change them at any time in your device settings. The rest of the app continues to work without them.

  • Camera: to take a photo to attach to a checklist item.
  • Photo library: to choose an existing photo to attach to a checklist item.
  • Microphone: to record a voice note on a checklist item.

Photos and recordings you attach are saved to your account so you can get to them again. The app does not access your camera, photos, or microphone in the background.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information only to run the app for you: to sign you in, to save and show your checklists, Files, photos, and recordings, and to keep your account secure. If you ask, the app can read the text in a photo you saved, so you can keep or copy it as words. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Data Storage and Security

Your account, checklists, and Files are stored on our server, separately for each account: only you can see them in the app. Information travels between the app and our server over encrypted connections. On the server, your information can be reached only by the small, named team that operates the app, and only where needed to run it and support you. We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no system is completely secure.

Reminders you set are scheduled locally on your device. They are not sent to or stored on our server.

The app can be used offline in read-only mode, showing information previously loaded on your device. Changes require an internet connection.

Third Parties

Your information is handled only by us and, as service providers, the company that hosts our server and the service that delivers our emails (invitations, password resets, and sign-in codes). They process information only to provide those services. We do not share your information with anyone else, and we may disclose information only if required by law.

Children's Privacy

Welcome Home is intended for adults. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. You may ask us to delete your account and everything tied to it — your profile, checklists, Files, photos, and recordings — by contacting us at the address below. We will delete it within a reasonable time, except where we must keep information to meet a legal obligation. Deletion is final and cannot be undone. Removing the app from your device also removes the reminders and other data stored on the device.

Your Rights

You may ask to see the information we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable law. To make a request, contact us below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the new version on this page and change the effective date above. If a change matters, we will also show the updated policy in the app. Please review it periodically.
