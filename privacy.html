Privacy Policy

Effective date: [Effective date: ____]

Welcome Home (listed in app stores as "MyWelcomeHome") is a free app that helps people returning to the community in Fairfield County, Connecticut find local resources, work through reentry checklists, and keep their own notes and reminders. The app is provided by [Organization legal name] ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

You need an account to use the app. We collect and store the following, tied to your account:

  • Account information: the details you provide to create and sign in to your account (such as an email address or username and a password).
  • Checklist progress and notes: the checklist items you complete and any text notes you write.
  • Photos: pictures you take with the camera or choose from your photo library to attach to a checklist item.
  • Voice recordings: audio you record as a voice note on a checklist item.

We do not use analytics or advertising tools, and this website does not use cookies or trackers.

How We Use It

We use your information only to run the app for you: to sign you in, to save and show your checklists, notes, photos, and recordings, and to keep your account secure. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Data Storage and Security

Your account, notes, photos, and voice recordings are stored on the app's server, separately for each account. Data is sent between the app and the server over encrypted connections. We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no system is completely secure.

Reminders you set are scheduled locally on your device. They are not sent to or stored on our server.

The app can be used offline in read-only mode, showing information previously loaded on your device. Changes require an internet connection.

Camera, Microphone, and Photos Permissions

The app asks for these permissions only when you use a related feature, and you can refuse or change them at any time in your device settings. The rest of the app continues to work without them.

  • Camera: to take a photo to attach to a checklist item.
  • Photo library: to choose an existing photo to attach to a checklist item.
  • Microphone: to record a voice note on a checklist item.

Photos and recordings you attach are uploaded to the server and stored with your account. The app does not access your camera, photos, or microphone in the background.

Third Parties

We do not share your information with third parties for marketing or advertising. Your data is handled only by the app's own server and the hosting provider that runs it. We do not use any other third-party services that receive your personal information. We may disclose information if required by law.

Children's Privacy

Welcome Home is intended for adults. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. You may ask us to delete your account and all associated data, including notes, photos, and recordings, by contacting us at the address below. We will delete it within a reasonable time, except where we must keep information to meet a legal obligation. Removing the app from your device also removes locally stored data and reminders.

Your Rights

You may ask to see the information we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable law. To make a request, contact us below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the new version on this page and change the effective date above. Please review it periodically.
